Clinical Review Summary

This summary is structured to help healthcare professionals reassess polypharmacy before prescribing additional therapy.

Priority concerns

1. Review the complete medication list and verify active indications.
2. Evaluate whether reported symptoms may be drug-induced.
3. Screen for therapeutic duplication and potential prescribing cascades.
4. Re-titrate or adjust regimen according to current organ function and age.

Medications to reassess

All active medications: confirm indication, therapeutic benefit, duration, and patient compliance. RxRecheck highlights areas for clinical evaluation without replacing clinical judgment.
